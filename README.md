## Metric formulas

These are general definitions. Exact results depend on the order-status filters, campaign attribution rules, and variable costs included.

| Metric | General formula |
| --- | --- |
| Gross sales | Sum of quantity × unit selling price |
| Net sales | Gross sales − discounts − refunds |
| Return on ad spend (ROAS) | Campaign-attributed revenue ÷ ad spend |
| COD return-to-origin rate | COD orders returned to origin ÷ eligible shipped COD orders × 100 |
| Contribution margin | Net sales − cost of goods sold − other variable costs |
| Contribution margin (%) | Contribution margin ÷ net sales × 100 |
